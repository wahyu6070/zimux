# Privacy Policy — zimux

**Last updated: 10 July 2026**

zimux ("the app", "we", "us") is a terminal emulator and file manager for
Android. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app handles and how. By
using zimux you agree to this policy.

## Summary

- zimux does **not** collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.
- There are **no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs**.
- Everything you do — files, terminal sessions, installed packages — stays
  **on your device**.

## Data We Collect

**None.** zimux does not create an account, does not ask for your identity, and
does not send your files, commands, keystrokes, or usage statistics to us or to
any server we control. We have no servers that receive your data.

## On-Device Data

The following data is stored only locally on your device and is never uploaded
by the app:

- **Files and folders** you browse, create, copy, move, extract, or delete with
  the file manager.
- **Terminal sessions** and the Linux environment (e.g. the Ubuntu rootfs
  installed via proot/chroot), including any files, packages, and command
  history you create inside it.
- **App settings** (theme, language, view options, remembered "open with"
  choices) saved in the app's private storage.

You can remove this data at any time by deleting the relevant files, clearing
the app's storage, or uninstalling the app.

## Permissions

zimux requests only the permissions it needs to work:

- **Storage / Manage all files** (`MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`,
  `READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`) — to let the file manager and terminal read and
  write files you choose. Files are accessed locally and never uploaded.
- **Internet & network state** (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`) — used only
  when **you** ask for it: downloading a Linux distribution (rootfs), installing
  packages with tools like `apt`, or when you run network commands (e.g. `curl`,
  `git`, `ssh`) inside the terminal. zimux itself sends no data.
- **Install packages** (`REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES`) — to let you install
  packages/APKs you explicitly open.
- **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) and **foreground service**
  (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC`) — to show progress for
  long-running file operations (copy, extract, archive) so the system does not
  interrupt them.

## Network & Third Parties

zimux has no backend of its own. When you download a Linux distribution or
install packages from within the terminal, your device connects **directly** to
the third-party servers you request (for example, distribution or package
mirrors). Those connections are subject to the privacy policies of whoever
operates those servers. zimux does not add tracking to these requests.

## Children

zimux is a general-purpose developer tool and is not directed at children. It
collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Security

Because your data never leaves your device through the app, its security depends
on your device's own protections (lock screen, encryption, etc.). Please keep
your device secure and be careful when granting the app storage access or
running commands from untrusted sources.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at
the URL where this policy is published, with an updated "Last updated" date.
